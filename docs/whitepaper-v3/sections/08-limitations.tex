\section{Limitations}\label{sec:limits}

\paragraph{Development data only.}
No confirmation, holdout or sealed panel was opened.
The report look used fresh row splits, but of the same DEV tables on which the encoder was designed and selected, so it says little about tables or lineages outside that set; adaptive reuse of a development set biases such estimates upward~\citep{dwork2015reusable}, in DOPE's favour and not the third-party generators'.
The pre-registration and its hash log are committed with the receipts; the schedule is identified in the receipts by hash and not published.

\paragraph{One look.}
The comparison with the third-party generators rests on this one look; it has not been repeated on other splits.
Its direction, DOPE against each generator, was re-reduced after scoring (\S\ref{sec:protocol}); the per-cell scores and intervals were not changed by it.

\paragraph{Third-party configurations.}
Each third-party generator was fitted once per cell with library defaults, no tuning and no byte cap (files scored up to \VThree{MethodsScoreBudget}~bytes), so it fails the byte guard by construction and its score reflects its own file sizes.
Tuned configurations, or other generators, may score differently.
Entries whose sample holds a single class score \VThree{FailedValue} by protocol; the run's report (\path{methods-report.md}) attributes TVAE's failed cells to such samples.
The comparisons are between these configurations on these splits, not the methods in general.

\paragraph{Small panel and an unfrozen contract.}
The look has \VThree{Families} informative families, as few as \VThree{BinaryFamilies} in one task.
In the four sign-flip checks, one per pair with \VThree{MethodsNullReplicates} replicates each, the statistical conditions passed in \VThree{MethodsStatPassMinPct} to \VThree{MethodsStatPassMaxPct} of replicates; these per-pair sign-flip pass rates are above the contract's threshold of \VThree{NullMaxRatePct}, although on their own they do not show that the percentile interval under-covers. The conditions together with the guards that move under a sign flip passed in at most \VThree{MethodsFlipMaxPct}.
These are diagnostics of each pair on its own, not a joint false-PROMOTE rate over the four.
Holm's adjustment covers the \VThree{MethodsHolmM} third-party pairs and nothing else scored on these splits.
The contract is a candidate and not frozen, so the rule itself may change before a confirmatory look.

\paragraph{Realism.}
The observed median C2ST AUCs are high for every generator scored here.
A boosted-tree discriminator separates DOPE's rows from real ones about as easily as rows with no dependence at all (median C2ST AUC \VThree{CandC2st} for both), and the third-party rows are separated about as easily (medians from \VThree{ArfC2st} to \VThree{CtganC2st}).
DOPE preserves more predictive structure for the auditors than independently permuted columns do, and CTGAN scores below that control (Table~\ref{tab:ranking}); nothing here supports using any of these rows where realism matters.

\paragraph{Privacy.}
Storing no source rows and clipping marginals at a support floor are design properties, not guarantees.
The privacy gate is an empirical audit (distance to closest record, a distance-based membership-inference attack, exact copies) on the rows scored; it is neither differential privacy~\citep{dwork2006calibrating} nor HIPAA de-identification, and passing it does not rule out other attacks~\citep{stadler2022groundhog}.

\paragraph{The release decision.}
Under the same contract, the pre-registered comparison of this encoder with the previous DOPE release returned HOLD: the guards on per-task loss, the held-out auditor, realism and privacy coverage missed.
That receipt is committed with the scoreboard; this paper does not rely on it.

\paragraph{Learned refinement.}
\texttt{DSE8} reserves room for a learned refinement of the analytic decode, trained from the FIT-only evidence bank of Appendix~\ref{app:evidence}.
No checkpoint has yet passed the promotion rule against the analytic packet, so the release ships no learned weights and \cli{auto-v8} without \cli{-{}-v8-weights} never writes a neural packet.

\paragraph{Provenance.}
The receipts record the scoring tool as \texttt{dope\_kpi} version \VThree{ToolVersion} (the tool's own version number, not a DOPE release) with build commit ``\VThree{ToolGitSha}'', and the scored packets are not committed, so the scores cannot be recomputed from the repository alone.
Before publication, the signed release binary re-emitted every scheduled packet of the report look, and the status, size and SHA-256 matched on every key; the release record lists this replay and its receipts~\citep{dope2026v3release}.
The replay shows that the published binary writes the packets that were scored; it does not recompute the scores, which need the private tables.
