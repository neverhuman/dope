\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Tabular generative models.}
Gaussian-copula synthesizers combine per-column marginals with a dependence model~\citep{sklar1959fonctions,patki2016sdv}.
CTGAN and TVAE fit adversarial and variational models with mode-specific normalization~\citep{xu2019ctgan}. Adversarial random forests alternate between a forest discriminator and a density estimate on its leaves~\citep{watson2023arf}. TabDDPM and TabSyn use diffusion, the latter in a learned latent space~\citep{kotelnikov2023tabddpm,zhang2024tabsyn}. ForestDiffusion estimates a diffusion score or a flow with gradient-boosted trees~\citep{jolicoeur2024forest}.
DOPE fixes the complete artifact size and measures how much downstream utility survives at that size.
We score SDV's Gaussian copula, CTGAN and TVAE and the ARF of arfpy under the same protocol, with their library defaults and no byte budget (\S\ref{sec:results}).

\paragraph{Table compression.}
Squish uses Bayesian networks and arithmetic coding for archival compression~\citep{gao2016squish}. DeepSqueeze encodes individual tuples with an autoencoder and supports error bounds for lossy numerical compression~\citep{ilkhechi2020deepsqueeze}. DOPE fits one target-aware generator for the table. Its objective is transfer to real test rows, and the artifact contains no encoding for an individual row.

\paragraph{Dataset distillation and coresets.}
Dataset distillation learns a few synthetic examples on which a model trains well~\citep{wang2018distillation}, and coresets select weighted subsets whose loss approximates the full data's~\citep{bachem2017coresets,mirzasoleiman2020craig}.
Both store examples, real or synthetic, and are budgeted in examples.
A DOPE artifact stores a parametric generator and no rows, and is budgeted in bytes of the whole file.

\paragraph{Utility, realism and privacy metrics.}
Train-on-synthetic, test-on-real (TSTR) scores a generator by the test loss of a model trained on its samples~\citep{esteban2017tstr}.
TRG (\S\ref{sec:protocol}) is a TSTR variant that normalizes by a train-on-real reference and subtracts the same quantity for a real twin the size of the fitting split, so that zero means ``as useful as fresh real data''.
Classifier two-sample tests (C2ST) measure whether synthetic and real rows can be told apart~\citep{lopezpaz2017c2st}; sample-level fidelity and coverage metrics refine this view~\citep{alaa2022faithful}.
Differential privacy gives formal guarantees~\citep{dwork2006calibrating}. MST and AIM fit synthetic generators using privately measured marginals~\citep{mckenna2021mst,mckenna2022aim}. Synthetic data without a formal mechanism can still leak membership~\citep{shokri2017membership,stadler2022groundhog}; DOPE measures empirical distance and membership attacks on its outputs.

\paragraph{Statistical protocol.}
Intervals come from a bootstrap~\citep{efron1979bootstrap} that resamples lineage families, the cluster bootstrap of \citet{davison1997bootstrap}; comparisons use Holm's step-down adjustment~\citep{holm1979simple}.
The report schedule and the decision rule were fixed before the reported look~\citep{nosek2018preregistration}; the third-party generators were added after the encoder was fixed, and the direction DOPE minus each generator was computed after scoring (\S\ref{sec:protocol}).
Because the same development tables were used for selection, the look is subject to the adaptive-reuse concerns studied by \citet{dwork2015reusable} (\S\ref{sec:limits}).
