Figure~\ref{fig:hero} and Tables~\ref{tab:ranking} and~\ref{tab:paired} describe the report look of \S\ref{sec:protocol}: \VThree{Scheduled} scheduled cells, of which \VThree{Uninformative} are uninformative on real data and leave every arm, and \VThree{CandCells} informative cells in \VThree{Families} families (\VThree{RegressionFamilies} regression, \VThree{BinaryFamilies} binary and \VThree{MulticlassFamilies} multiclass).
Every generator was fitted on the same rows $A$ of each cell and scored by the same auditors on the same test rows; Appendix~\ref{app:arms} gives the third-party configurations.

\paragraph{Ranking.}
At \VThree{PrimaryMult}$\times$ sampling, DOPE reaches a Transfer Score of \VThree{CandScore}.
ARF follows at \VThree{ArfScore}, then the Gaussian copula at \VThree{GcScore}, TVAE at \VThree{TvaeScore} and CTGAN at \VThree{CtganScore}.
For reference, a copy of the fitting rows scores \VThree{CopyAScorePoint}, not \VThree{ScoreScale}: each cell keeps the auditor on which the real twin does better than the sample, so even other real rows score a little below \VThree{ScoreScale}. A bootstrap resample of the fitting rows scores \VThree{BootAScorePoint}.
Permuting every column independently scores \VThree{IndepScorePoint} and permuting only the target \VThree{LabelShuffleScorePoint}; CTGAN scores below both.

\paragraph{Paired differences.}
DOPE is ahead of the Gaussian copula by \VThree{GcVsPts} points, of TVAE by \VThree{TvaeVsPts} and of CTGAN by \VThree{CtganVsPts}; each interval is above zero.
Against ARF the difference is \VThree{ArfVsPts} points, and its interval contains zero: on this look DOPE is not shown to transfer better than ARF.
The two differ by task.
ARF is higher on regression (\VThree{ArfRegressionScore} against \VThree{CandRegressionScore}); DOPE is higher on binary (\VThree{CandBinaryScore} against \VThree{ArfBinaryScore}) and multiclass targets (\VThree{CandMulticlassScore} against \VThree{ArfMulticlassScore}).

\paragraph{Matched size.}
With a synthetic sample the size of $A$ ($m=\VThree{MatchedMult}$), the order is unchanged: DOPE \VThree{CandMatchedScorePoint}, ARF \VThree{ArfMatchedScorePoint}, Gaussian copula \VThree{GcMatchedScorePoint}, TVAE \VThree{TvaeMatchedScorePoint} and CTGAN \VThree{CtganMatchedScorePoint}.
The difference from ARF is \VThree{ArfVsPtsMatched} points.

\paragraph{Size.}
Every DOPE file is within the budget: the median is \VThree{CandBytesMed}~bytes and the largest \VThree{CandBytesMax}.
The third-party files have median sizes from \VThree{GcBytesMed}~bytes (Gaussian copula) to \VThree{ArfBytesMed}~bytes (ARF), \VThree{MethodsRatioMin} to \VThree{MethodsRatioMax} times DOPE's median, and none is within the budget.
Their scores are therefore those of unconstrained files; held to the budget, each would score the floor value \VThree{FailedValue} on every entry.

\paragraph{Realism, privacy and copies.}
Realism coverage is \VThree{CandRealism} for DOPE, lower than ARF's \VThree{ArfRealism} and higher than the Gaussian copula's \VThree{GcRealism}; TVAE and CTGAN pass the realism gate on no entry.
Privacy coverage is \VThree{CandPrivacy} for DOPE; CTGAN is higher at \VThree{CtganPrivacy}, the Gaussian copula and TVAE are lower (\VThree{GcPrivacy} and \VThree{TvaePrivacy}), and ARF passes the privacy gate on few entries (\VThree{ArfPrivacy}).
CTGAN alone exceeds the exact-copy bound.
No generator produces realistic rows: the median C2ST AUC is \VThree{CandC2st} for DOPE, the same as for independently permuted columns, and between \VThree{ArfC2st} and \VThree{CtganC2st} for the third-party generators, against \VThree{CopyAC2st} for a copy of the fitting rows.

\paragraph{The full promotion rule.}
Table~\ref{tab:paired} also applies the contract's promotion rule to each pair with DOPE as the candidate.
DOPE is \VThree{TvaeVsRule} against TVAE, with every guard passing.
Against the Gaussian copula and CTGAN the transfer conditions hold but one guard misses (the Gaussian copula's per-task lower bound and CTGAN's privacy coverage), and against ARF the interval contains zero and three guards miss, so these three pairs are \VThree{GcVsRule}.

\paragraph{Failures.}
TVAE fails \VThree{TvaeFailed} of \VThree{TvaeCells} cells, where its sample holds a single class and the entry scores \VThree{FailedValue} by protocol; DOPE and the other generators score every cell.
