\begin{abstract}
\noindent When an outside team cannot take possession of a table, it can be difficult to test a new modeling pipeline on the task that matters. DOPE fits a table and a declared prediction target into a file of at most \VThree{BudgetBytes}~bytes. The file stores no complete fitting record and generates synthetic training rows for a learner chosen by the recipient. Transfer Score measures prediction on held-out real rows on a scale where a real training twin scores \VThree{ScoreScale}.
On fresh row splits of development tables (\VThree{CandCells} informative cells in \VThree{Families} lineage families), DOPE~\VThree{ReleaseVersion} scores \VThree{CandScore} at \VThree{PrimaryMult}$\times$ sampling from a median file of \VThree{CandBytesMed}~bytes. A uniform subset of fitting rows stored and resampled under the same cap scores \CapScore{\RowsBudget}{score} [\CapScore{\RowsBudget}{low}, \CapScore{\RowsBudget}{high}].
DOPE has the highest report point estimate and the smallest artifact among the tested generators. Paired intervals favor DOPE over three SDV configurations on each of three development split sets. The interval against ARF includes zero on each.
We describe the fitting-only statistics, learner landmarks, and the learned refinement pilot that did not ship. These are development estimates. A discriminator readily separates the samples from real rows, and the privacy audits are empirical.
\end{abstract}
