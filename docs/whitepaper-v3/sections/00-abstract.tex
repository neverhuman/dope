\begin{abstract}
\noindent DOPE compiles a table with a declared target into a small file from which any number of synthetic rows can be sampled; the file stores no source rows.
Release~\VThree{ReleaseVersion} adds \cli{dope fit -{}-format auto-v8}, which fits two candidates inside one budget of \VThree{BudgetBytes}~bytes for the whole file: the Format~1 program of earlier releases and \texttt{DSE8}, a new analytic packet with an explicit byte model.
It keeps \texttt{DSE8} only when a duplicate-grouped, target-stratified two-fold check on the fitting rows prefers it by a fixed margin without a large loss on either fold.
We describe the \texttt{DSE8} codec and its byte accounting, the analytic fit, a FIT-only evidence bank for learned encoders, and the \texttt{dope\_kpi} protocol, which scores a generator by the twin-referenced gap (TRG): the retention of downstream auditors trained on synthetic rows minus that of auditors trained on a real twin the size of the fitting split.
On one pre-registered look at fresh row splits of the development tables (\VThree{CandCells} informative cells in \VThree{Families} lineage families; no confirmation panel), \texttt{auto-v8} reaches a Transfer Score, $\VThree{ScoreScale}\,(1+\mathrm{TRG})$, at \VThree{PrimaryMult}$\times$ sampling of \VThree{CandScorePoint} points against \VThree{BaseScorePoint} for the release~\VThree{BaseVersion} encoder, a paired gain of \VThree{DeltaPts} points (TRG \VThree{Delta}, Holm $p=\VThree{PHolm}$), at a median of \VThree{CandBytesMed}~bytes.
The contract's promotion rule nonetheless returns \textbf{\VThree{Outcome}}: \VThree{GuardsFailed} of its \VThree{GuardsTotal} guards fail (the regression lower bound, the held-out MLP auditor, realism coverage and empirical-privacy coverage), the matched-size gain's interval contains zero, and the statistical conditions alone pass in \VThree{StatPassPct} of sign-flip null replicates.
Four third-party generators run with library defaults and no byte cap (SDV's Gaussian copula, CTGAN and TVAE, and ARF) all have a lower TRG than \texttt{auto-v8}, none within the budget; the closest, ARF, differs by \VThree{ArfVsDelta} (\VThree{ArfVsOutcome}), and the other three are REJECT.
Synthetic rows remain easy to tell from real ones: the median classifier two-sample AUC is \VThree{CandC2st}, the same as for independently permuted columns.
A second pre-registered look (data seeds \VThree{FlSeeds}) again finds the released engine HOLD against release~\VThree{BaseVersion}; a candidate build that was PROMOTE there against that release but not above the released engine did not ship.
The empirical privacy diagnostics are neither differential privacy nor HIPAA de-identification.
\end{abstract}
