\paragraph{Addendum F and the final look.}
Addendum~F admitted a build to the final look only if, on wide DEV (data seeds \VThree{WideSeeds}), it was PROMOTE against release~\VThree{BaseVersion} and its point difference against \VThree{FlServedBuild} was at least zero.
Of the \VThree{WideBuilds} builds tried, \VThree{WideSixId} was \VThree{WideSixOutcome} (privacy coverage \VThree{WideSixPrivacy} fails its guard) and \VThree{WideSixbId} was \VThree{WideSixbOutcome} at \VThree{WideSixbDelta}, with Holm $p=\VThree{WideSixbPHolm}$ over the builds and a difference of \VThree{WideSixbVsCand} against \VThree{FlServedBuild}; its gain there also came from \VThree{WideSixbNonzero} of \VThree{WideSixbFamilies} families (sign-flip $p=\VThree{WideSixbSignFlipP}$).
Under the scoreboard directory, the eligibility receipts are in \path{evidence/selection/wide-dev/} and the look's in \path{evidence/final-look-s9-12/} (primary \texttt{\VThree{FlPrimarySha}}, family \texttt{\VThree{FlFamilySha}}).
The renderer requires every look file to match \path{SHA256SUMS}; the look to be scored by the frozen \texttt{dope\_kpi} build under the frozen schedule and contract; the pre-registration's freeze to name that schedule, the candidate's source and the three binaries, and its result to name the look's receipts; and its hash log to end with the committed pre-registration's SHA-256. It recomputes the Holm family, the family sums and the sign-flip $p$ values.
The descriptive comparison of the released engine with release~\VThree{BaseVersion} was scored without the families file, so its panel lacks the families hash; its two arms equal those of the look's primary receipt field for field.

\paragraph{Third-party configuration.}
Each method ran with its library defaults (no hyperparameter was passed or tuned) on CPU with one thread, through a wrapper that fits once per cell on $A$ and samples once per multiplier.
SDV detects the feature types, and the target is declared categorical or numerical by task.
A file is the pickled fitted object: SDV's own save, and for ARF the fitted object without its copy of the fitting rows and its forest, neither of which the sampler reads.
No byte cap was applied, and the files were scored with a byte limit of \VThree{MethodsScoreBudget}.
The run manifest (\path{MANIFEST.json}, SHA-256 prefix \texttt{\VThree{MethodsManifestSha}}) records the software: Python~\VThree{MethodsPythonVersion}, SDV~\VThree{SdvVersion} with CTGAN~\VThree{CtganVersion}, copulas~\VThree{CopulasVersion} and RDT~\VThree{RdtVersion}, arfpy~\VThree{ArfpyVersion}, PyTorch~\VThree{TorchVersion} and scikit-learn~\VThree{SklearnVersion}, with every recipe and command.
SDV and its libraries are licensed \VThree{SdvLicense}; arfpy is licensed \VThree{ArfpyLicense}.
The run labels these arms \texttt{source\_free} after an audit of \VThree{MethodsAuditFiles} smoke-run files per method (the run scored \VThree{MethodsScheduledCells} cells per method) found no stored table of fitting rows and no copy of a fitting column.
The files do keep per-column ranges, category values and fitted transformer parameters (SDV) and per-leaf means, deviations, bounds and class probabilities (ARF).
