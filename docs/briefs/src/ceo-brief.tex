  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{The Trillion-Dollar Data-Lock Paradox}
  {\fontsize{8.45}{10.5}\selectfont
  Most enterprise data worth learning from never leaves the systems that collected it. Clinical records, insurance claims, banking ledgers, pricing tables, and industrial telemetry sit behind strict regulatory, contractual, and competitive firewalls --- while the models and external AI vendors that could create value sit outside.

  \vspace{2.5pt}
  Today, leaders face three broken choices: \textbf{(1)~export records}, triggering months of legal review and irreversible transfer risk; \textbf{(2)~trust vendor demos on public data}, buying blind on tables unlike your business; or \textbf{(3)~build every model internally}, paying twice in talent and time-to-market. Most high-value vendor evaluations are never attempted.
  }
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.486\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{The Paradigm Shift: Ship a 2\,KiB Compiler Output}
  {\fontsize{8.45}{10.5}\selectfont
  \textbf{\wordmark{} (Downstream Objective-Preserving Encoding)} reframes tabular AI evaluation as \emph{extreme generative compression}. A signed pure-Rust executable compiles your table and one declared prediction target (churn, credit risk, claims cost, yield) inside your firewall into a \textbf{$\le$\,\VThree{BudgetBytes}-byte generative program} with every stored bucket backed by at least \VThree{SupportK} fitting rows.

  \vspace{2.5pt}
  Any number of internal teams or external AI vendors draw synthetic training rows from that tiny file, train and tune models normally, and return finished models --- which \textbf{your team scores back inside on untouched real holdout rows}. One approved 2\,KiB file turns a six-month impasse into a parallel multi-vendor bake-off.
  }
\end{minipage}

\briefsection{03}{How to Pilot Safely in 30 Days --- and What Governance Must Know}

\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.478\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{3-Step Low-Risk Enterprise Pilot}
  \vspace{1pt}
  {\fontsize{7.8}{9.8}\selectfont\raggedright
  \noindent\stepbadge{1}\;\textbf{Compile inside on one approved table (Day 1).}\\
  Select one internally approved tabular problem with a clear business target. Run the signed offline \tcli{dope} CPU binary inside your firewall to emit a $\le$\,\VThree{BudgetBytes}-byte file in seconds. (On our public UCI Airfoil walkthrough, a 46.5\,KB CSV compiles to 2,044\,B and retains 0.971--0.977 held-out accuracy in 5.5 seconds.)

  \vspace{3pt}
  \noindent\stepbadge{2}\;\textbf{Run a blind build outside (Weeks 1--2).}\\

    \textbf{Enterprise rigor requires clear boundaries. Release \VThree{ReleaseVersion} carries five explicit limits:}
    \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.10em,itemsep=1.6pt,topsep=2pt]
      \item \textbf{Derived data --- Legal decides:} Treat \tcli{.dope} files and synthetic CSVs as \emph{derived data}. Fitted schema and summaries can carry source information; compliance governs boundary release.
      \item \textbf{No formal differential privacy \& not HIPAA de-identification:} Release \VThree{ReleaseVersion} implements \textbf{no formal differential privacy mechanism} and is \textbf{not HIPAA de-identification} (Safe Harbor or Expert Determination). Privacy checks are empirical diagnostics (\VThree{CandPrivacy} pass rate, 0.000 exact-copy excess, $k=\VThree{SupportK}$ floor).
      \item \textbf{Uncertified DEV-SELECT estimates:} Published scores come from fresh development splits. No confirmation, holdout, or sealed certification panel has been opened (\texttt{docs/release.md}).
      \item \textbf{Statistical nuance vs.\ ARF:} While DOPE's point estimate leads ARF by +10.8 points from a 1,644\x{} smaller file, the 95\% paired interval (\VThree{ArfVsPts}) includes zero; DOPE is not shown to outperform ARF on transfer alone.
      \item \textbf{Built for training models, not realistic rows:} A boosted-tree discriminator still distinguishes DOPE rows from real rows easily (median C2ST AUC \textbf{\VThree{CandC2st}}). Cross-model feature-driver preservation is unmeasured.
