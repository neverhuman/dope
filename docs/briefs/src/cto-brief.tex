  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{Dual-Codec Design \& Parser Hardening}
  {\fontsize{8.05}{10.0}\selectfont
  Why does \tcli{auto-v8} outperform either codec alone? Format\,1 compresses dense continuous and multiclass structure well (73.3) but fails 7/44 cells under tight budgets; analytic \tcli{DSE8} never fails (0/44) and excels on regression (90.6) via closed-form mandatory sizing and utility-ordered optional prefix truncation without refitting. Together they hit \textbf{87.9} with \textbf{0/44 failures}.

  \vspace{2.5pt}
  Every parser fails closed: \tcli{DSE8} rejects unknown magic/version/flags/tags, duplicate or out-of-order TLV tags, truncation, trailing bytes, missing mandatory frames, and CRC-32 mismatches. Format\,1 enforces 64\,MiB inflation/materialization caps, 1\,MiB JSON header limits, 4,096-feature bounds, and acyclic tree checks against decompression bombs. Errors and public sidecars never echo source values, headers, or extrema.
  }
\end{minipage}\hfill
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.486\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \colheading{FIT-Only Evidence Bank (\tcli{evidence-layout-v1})}
  {\fontsize{8.05}{10.0}\selectfont
  DOPE's owner-side featurization is pure, single-threaded, deterministic, uncached Rust whose input struct has \textbf{no query-row field by construction}. It emits row-count-independent masked tensors --- \textbf{\VThree{EvColDim}} col / \textbf{\VThree{EvEdgeDim}} pair / \textbf{\VThree{EvGlobDim}} global slots (+16/class, 4/class-col, 8/group) across \textbf{4 tiers}:
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.12em,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textbf{$T_0$:} 1 sort/col --- $k{=}10$ support floor, 17-knot quantile residuals, L-moments/tails, missingness, task/size.
    \item \textbf{$T_1$:} Rank-12 factor/precision copula, tail/directional dependence, 16 pair channels (product/XOR target terms), 3-fold OOF IRLS.
    \item \textbf{$T_2$:} 2-fold depth-3 GBDT witness, Walsh--Hadamard functional ANOVA, calibration, 1-NN noise floor.
    \item \textbf{$T_3$:} Uncached closed-loop packet probes (AUC, correlation gap, DCR share; masked unless a packet is passed).
  \end{itemize}
  }

  \briefsection{02}{Paired Statistics \& Why \tcli{SingleEncV8} Did Not Ship}
  {\fontsize{7.85}{9.9}\selectfont
  \textbf{Paired contrasts (4\x, Holm-adjusted):} Against \textbf{ARF}, DOPE's paired difference is \textbf{\VThree{ArfVsPts}} (Holm $p=0.114$) $\rightarrow$ \textbf{HOLD} (missed tasks, MLP, realism); the 95\% interval includes zero across all three DEV split sets, so DOPE is not shown to transfer better than ARF on this look. Against SDV, DOPE is \textbf{PROMOTE} vs.\ TVAE (\VThree{TvaeVsPts}) and \textbf{HOLD} vs.\ Gaussian copula (\VThree{GcVsPts}, tasks) and CTGAN (\VThree{CtganVsPts}, privacy).

  \vspace{2.5pt}
  \textbf{Transparent neural pilot result:} We trained a learned encoder-decoder (\tcli{SingleEncV8}) over the Evidence Bank on real tables, target swaps, and synthetic priors (3 runs $\times$ 8,000 updates on RTX\,4090). It lowered training loss and screening error, but scored \textbf{\m0.37} (95\% CI \textbf{[\m2.71, +1.72]}) on fresh DEV splits and missed task/MLP guards. Release \VThree{ReleaseVersion} therefore ships weight-free --- and \texttt{ROADMAP.md} invites external contributors to enrich the Evidence Bank with data-specific features (with co-authorship on future releases).
  }
