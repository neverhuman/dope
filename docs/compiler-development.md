# Bounded compiler development mechanisms

These mechanisms operate on permitted fitting data. They do not consume final
query outcomes, issue certification or establish improvement on independent
sources and learners.

The symbolic tournament gives each target/dependence family a round-robin
opportunity within the existing beam budget. Learned priorities order variants
within a family. Every promoted variant is encoded and decoded before measuring
its train-only target and driver diagnostics; the final file bytes enforce the
cap. `search_history` retains encoded acceptances, actual over-cap rejections,
time and deadline-unvisited states. Unvisited families are not inferior results.
The raw-unit envelope reserves its retained transform/schema/framing charge
before allocating a native-payload cap.

`compile --adaptive-allocation` opts into development racing over the same fitted
target/dependence pool, knot/precision ladder and offer budget. Frozen empirical
`--candidate` profiles refuse this mode. Families receive an initial opportunity
before racing; one of every four later offers explores the least visited family.
Encoded observations maintain quality/byte tradeoffs and comparison parents.
Only exact zero support is pruned after quantization, preserving nonzero fitted
signals. Every offered encoded rejection remains charged, shared fitting time is
separate, and unvisited beam/deadline candidates remain unavailable. The exploration
bonus is a heuristic. It is not calibrated uncertainty, an attributable transfer
gain, or qualification of unsupported mask/architecture refinements.

Ga2m fitting now consumes an independent pair screen. Up to 64 predictors it
tests every pair; wider inputs receive a fixed, bounded 2,048-proposal exploration
budget. Four row-index-hash folds are independent of target values and candidate
choices. Thresholds and cell means are fitted on the fold complement, and gains
are normalized by the corresponding null squared error. Pair parents need not
have marginal target correlation. Proposals are limited to four actual encoded
interaction tables. Synthetic tests cover a pure pair at columns 30/31 among
40 predictors, shifted positive values, target units and column permutations,
and the actual compiler/encoded artifact call graph. Broader independent-source
benefit, rare-regime coverage and equal-compute frontier gains are unmeasured.

The preserved `ee533ef3a3394b66571d7c42b64854da0ee88876` PR19 prototype stays
on its original branch. This implementation supersedes its target-dependent
fold hashing, marginal-parent requirement and hypothetical 12/14/20-byte
tariffs; no old whole-branch cherry-pick or historical result import occurred.

Declared `Task` governs each target head and residual model. A continuous
regression target with two observed values remains regression. Complex native
regression heads estimate residuals using three fixed row folds over at most
256 permitted outer-fit rows, refitting that head family on each complement.
The outer-fit transformed view and subset/full-fit discrepancy are explicit.
Gaussian-reference log-absolute residual fitting includes
`(EulerGamma + log 2)/2`; quantile-based scale floors and bounds remain measured
model choices. Small samples, the historical homoscedastic linear baseline,
binary calibration and unavailable GPU refits retain distinct full-fit diagnostic
states. No extra GPU training is triggered. `residual_fitting` records these
states in the compiler report; every state remains unqualified for conditional
calibration, tails or privacy until separate evidence establishes it.

Historical DPK3.2 and V1 decoding and sampling remain intact. New raw-state
sampling removes the historical regression target clamp while retaining the
declared feature support limit. It counts numeric rounding repairs and refuses
physical-domain violations without silent clipping or draw replacement.
