\section{Question}
The object of this draft is a rate-risk compiler for numeric regression.
A decoded generator is selected on a development portfolio of learners.
Serialized bytes are then allocated where that portfolio shows transfer sensitivity.
Once receipts are bound, the reported quantity is original-unit loss on source families and learner families that were withheld from selection.
Bytes are a constraint.
They are not a term folded into the loss.

Two statements stay distinct.
The standard transfer bound is an assumption.
It limits how far withheld loss may move from development loss by a complexity term that does not depend on a new selection rule.
It is not a finding.
Its width is \TBD{standard-transfer-bound}.

The algorithmic hypothesis is the claim under test.
Development-only selection of the decoded program, together with byte allocation by development transfer sensitivity, changes withheld original-unit loss relative to the predeclared controls.
The signed change is \TBD{algorithmic-hypothesis}.
The margin that would count as support is recorded separately.
It is \TBD{algorithmic-hypothesis-margin}.

Until those placeholders are replaced from bound receipts, the hypothesis is untested.

Cross-source change is \TBD{cross-source-transfer}.

Cross-learner change is \TBD{cross-learner-transfer}.

The byte-constrained frontier is \TBD{rate-risk-frontier}.
