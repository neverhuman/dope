\title{Regression Utility of a 10{,}240-Byte Tabular Generator}

\author{The DOPE Project}

\markboth{The DOPE Project}%
{Regression Utility of a 10240-Byte Tabular Generator}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
The displayed DOPE generator is a compact residual: at most 12 screened
inputs, a width-16 $\tanh$ hidden layer, and a refit linear readout.
AdamW trains the hidden basis for 2{,}048 steps at learning rate $0.002$,
with no embedding. Each method keeps its own selection objective. After
selection, artifacts are scored by retention, the share of real-versus-null
validation improvement recovered by an auditor trained on synthetic rows,
and by charged bytes. On $\NDopeCb$ paired PMLB regression lineages the
CatBoost median is $\MedDopeCb$ (95\% lineage-bootstrap interval
$\LoDopeCb$ to $\HiDopeCb$), against $\MedGaussCb$ for a native-selected
Gaussian copula, $\MedChowCb$ for a study Chow--Liu tree, and $\MedIndCb$
for study independent marginals. Holm-adjusted Wilcoxon tests separate
these three CatBoost comparisons. The CatBoost record against the copula
is \WtlGaussCb\ wins/ties/losses. The linear auditor does not separate
DOPE from the Gaussian copula (\WtlLinGauss). An unpooled
$\NNeuCb$-lineage block compares CTGAN and TVAE. An unpooled
$\NForestCb$-lineage Forest-Flow block is ahead on CatBoost
(\WtlForestCb) at $\ForestByteLo$ to $\ForestByteHi$ bytes.
$\DopeWithinCap$ displayed DOPE artifacts sit inside the 10{,}240-byte cap.
Official tests were not opened. Master Fitness v2 is null. TabSyn, a
100-lineage TabDDPM panel, the production privacy-attack panel, fit-seed
variance, and the full ablation grid are not measured.
Table~\ref{tab:fidelity} is grouped-validation fidelity and an empirical
distance ledger, not differential privacy or HIPAA de-identification.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
A generator paper often reports the metric that also selected the
configuration. Those native scores live on different scales: predictive
efficacy for CTGAN and TVAE~\cite{xu2019ctgan}, held-out log density for a
Gaussian copula~\cite{patki2016sdv}, a forest density for
ARF~\cite{watson2023arf}, and a diffusion or CART objective for other
families~\cite{kotelnikov2023tabddpm,nowok2016synthpop}. This paper separates
selection from the common measurement. After each method has been selected
on its own objective, the shared columns are retention and the bytes charged
to the artifact.

The decision those columns can support is narrow. It is whether a network
small enough to ship under a 10{,}240-byte cap still preserves downstream
regression utility on real validation rows. It is not a declaration that one
generator is best, and it is not a certification score. Master Fitness v2
(MFS-v2) is defined in Section~\ref{sec:method} and is null on every method
here, because the six components and the hard-gate map are not all present.

The contributions are:
\begin{enumerate}
\item The displayed residual and the sealed-test training pipeline
(Section~\ref{sec:method}, Fig.~\ref{fig:arch}, \eqref{eq:residual}).
\item Retention, charged bytes, and grouped-validation fidelity, with
lineage-bootstrap intervals
(Fig.~\ref{fig:bytes}, Tables~\ref{tab:density}, \ref{tab:bytes}, and~\ref{tab:fidelity}).
\item Paired Wilcoxon tests with Holm correction, rank-biserial
correlations, and a Friedman--Nemenyi diagram on the common lineage set
(Fig.~\ref{fig:pairs}, Table~\ref{tab:density}).
\item A separate six-lineage Forest-Flow block
(Table~\ref{tab:forest}).
\item An explicit account of what this panel does not measure, including
MFS-v2 (Table~\ref{tab:score} and Section~\ref{sec:threats}).
\end{enumerate}
An unexecuted method is not a win for DOPE. Density, neural, Forest-Flow,
and BeyondArena blocks are not pooled.

\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
Tabular synthesis has moved from parametric copulas~\cite{patki2016sdv} and
Chow--Liu trees~\cite{chow1968dependence} through conditional GANs and
variational autoencoders~\cite{xu2019ctgan} to diffusion and score-based
models~\cite{kotelnikov2023tabddpm,kim2023stasy,lee2023codi,zhang2024tabsyn,shi2025tabdiff,mueller2025cdtd,jolicoeurmartineau2024forestdiffusion}.
Autoregressive and language-model generators~\cite{borisov2023great,solatorio2023realtabformer},
prior-data fitted generators~\cite{ma2023tabpfgen,hollmann2025tabpfn},
CTAB-GAN and CTAB-GAN+~\cite{zhao2021ctabgan,zhao2024ctabganplus}, and
graphical neural generators~\cite{liu2023goggle} target the same mixed-type
tables. ARF estimates density with a forest~\cite{watson2023arf}. CART
synthesis is the synthpop line~\cite{nowok2016synthpop}. A recent survey
collects these design choices~\cite{borisov2024survey}. This paper runs the
Gaussian copula, study Chow--Liu, study independent marginals, CTGAN, TVAE,
and a $\NForestCb$-lineage Forest-Flow confirmation. TabSyn, TabDDPM on the
$\PreparedN$-lineage panel, STaSy, CoDi, TabDiff, CTAB-GAN+, GReaT,
REaLTabFormer, and TabPFGen are cited and not measured. SMOTE~\cite{chawla2002smote} was not run.
This panel scores regression retention, not classification oversampling.

Differentially private Bayesian networks and marginal
synthesizers~\cite{zhang2014privbayes,mckenna2021mst,mckenna2022aim} optimize
a privacy budget. Membership inference and shadow-model attacks are a
separate detection literature~\cite{shokri2017membership,hayes2018logan,vanbreugel2023domias}.
Formal differential privacy is a different claim from an empirical
resemblance score~\cite{dwork2014foundations,abadi2016deep}. Utility reviews
separate marginal fidelity from downstream
prediction~\cite{snoke2018utility,raab2018practical}. Alpha-precision,
beta-recall, SDMetrics, and synthcity are the usual fidelity
reports~\cite{alaa2022faithful,zhang2020sdmetrics,qian2023synthcity}. The
density cells already store a classifier two-sample statistic, a mean
marginal Kolmogorov--Smirnov distance, and a pairwise correlation fidelity.
Those fields are summarized below. Table~\ref{tab:fidelity} adds
alpha-precision, beta-recall, a distance-to-closest-record share, and a
DOMIAS AUC on the grouped validation split. ARF is absent because shared
validation sampling is still pending. The production privacy-attack panel
remains \PrivacyAttackPanel. Nothing in those fields is a formal privacy
guarantee or a HIPAA de-identification claim.

Tree models remain strong tabular predictors, which is why CatBoost is one
of the three auditors~\cite{grinsztajn2022trees,prokhorenkova2018catboost}.
TabZilla and TabArena show how brittle a single split can
be~\cite{mcelfresh2023tabzilla,erickson2026tabarena}. The regression
lineages come from PMLB~\cite{olson2017pmlb,romano2021pmlb}. Comparing
several methods on the same lineages needs a rank test and a critical
difference, or a Bayesian signed-rank alternative~\cite{demsar2006statistical,benavoli2017time}.
This paper reports the frequentist procedure: a Friedman test and a Nemenyi
critical difference on the common set, plus paired Wilcoxon tests. The
Bayesian signed-rank procedure is not computed.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\subsection{Displayed generator}
The displayed profile is \texttt{features12\_\allowbreak{}steps2048}. It is a compact
neural residual, not a GAN, a diffusion model, or a transformer.
Fig.~\ref{fig:arch} is the pipeline. Training screens at most 12 inputs. A
hidden layer of width 16 maps them through $\tanh$. AdamW, learning rate
$0.002$, runs for 2{,}048 steps on the regression mean-squared error, with
gradient clipping at $5$. The hidden weights and biases are then frozen.
The intercept, the linear skip, and the hidden readout are refit on those
frozen features. Coefficients whose absolute value is below $10^{-5}$ are
dropped from the skip. For a selected input vector $x_S$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:residual}
h_j = \tanh\!\left(b_j + w_j^{\top} x_S\right),
\quad
\hat{y} = a + \beta^{\top} x_S + v^{\top} h .
\end{equation}
Sampling adds the noise model stored in the artifact. There is no embedding
table in \eqref{eq:residual}. A separate dataset-action router uses a vector
of dimension $856 + 24\times(128+10) = 4{,}168$. That vector does not
generate the rows in this paper.

The profile was fixed before the shared retention was inspected. Compile-time
search records the proxy $0.45u + 0.25d + 0.30j$ and then keeps the candidate
with the smallest normalized gate shortfall, then the smallest artifact, then
the lowest candidate id. Every such compile is marked
\texttt{production\_evidence\_unmeasured}. No global DOPE family has been
selected. Other shapes exist and are not this result: micro-TVAEs, TinyMAT,
joint TVAE, a masked transformer, and a TabSyn candidate. A direct-rank
TabDDPM candidate is a research comparator with no claim on the
10{,}240-byte tier. The scored campaign did not store a per-step loss. A
later logged replay did. Section~\ref{sec:ablation} plots that replay. The
replay files are not substituted for the scored artifacts, and a lower
hidden-basis loss at 8{,}192 steps does not replace the displayed profile.
The full width-and-step grid is \FullAblationGrid.

\subsection{Retention and MFS-v2}
Let $L_{\mathrm{null}}$, $L_{\mathrm{real}}$, and
$L_{\mathrm{synthetic}}$ be the auditor's loss on real validation rows when
that auditor is trained on a null baseline, on real fit rows, and on
synthetic rows. Retention is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:retention}
\frac{L_{\mathrm{null}}-L_{\mathrm{synthetic}}}{L_{\mathrm{null}}-L_{\mathrm{real}}} .
\end{equation}
A value of $1$ means the synthetic-trained auditor matched the real-trained
auditor. A value above $1$ means it beat that auditor. A negative value means
it did worse than the null. A lineage is informative when the real
improvement is at least $0.01$ times the absolute null loss and is not zero.
Otherwise the lineage is omitted. It is not entered as zero. The three
auditors are CatBoost~\cite{prokhorenkova2018catboost}, ordinary linear
regression, and an MLP. The lineage score is the median of sample seeds
$101$, $211$, and $307$. The panel score is the median of those lineage
scores. Intervals below are percentile lineage-bootstrap intervals,
10{,}000 resamples of lineages, with the three seeds kept inside the lineage
median. They are not per-seed whiskers.

MFS-v2 is a different object. With component $c_i\in[0,1]$, weight $w_i>0$,
$\epsilon=10^{-6}$, and $W=\sum_i w_i$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mfs}
\mathrm{MFS\text{-}v2}
= 100\exp\!\left(\frac{1}{W}\sum_i w_i\ln(\epsilon+c_i)\right)
\end{equation}
when the hard-gate map is non-empty, every hard gate passed, and every
positive-weight component is finite and inside $[0,1]$. Otherwise the scalar
is null. The weights are utility transfer $0.30$, driver fidelity $0.20$,
distribution fidelity $0.20$, structure fidelity $0.15$, coverage realism
$0.10$, and compactness $0.05$. A privacy failure removes the score through
a gate. Utility transfer inside \eqref{eq:mfs} is a clamped one-sided lower
bound, not the median of \eqref{eq:retention}. Compactness is
$\mathrm{clamp}(1 - \mathrm{bytes}/10240,\, 0,\, 1)$. Crossing 10{,}240
bytes fails the byte gate, so the scalar stays null. An artifact over the
cap is a failed gate, not a retention of zero.

Table~\ref{tab:illustration} applies \eqref{eq:mfs} to six invented
components $(0.90,\allowbreak 0.80,\allowbreak 0.85,\allowbreak 0.70,\allowbreak 0.75,\allowbreak 0.95)$. The resulting $82.40$ is
arithmetic, not a measurement. The second row fails the byte gate and the
implementation returns null. No artifact in this paper has a measured
MFS-v2 value.

\subsection{Statistics}
Comparisons are paired on the lineages where both methods have a complete
informative group. The tests are two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on the
nonzero paired differences, and one-sided sign tests for a positive
difference, with a matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation.
Holm correction is applied inside a family, separately for each test, and is
not pooled across families. On CatBoost the Holm sign-test values are
$\SignHolmGaussCb$, $\SignHolmChowCb$, and $\SignHolmIndCb$.
The density family has nine tests (three comparators times three auditors).
The neural family is CTGAN and TVAE times the auditors. The Forest-Flow
family is separate and has six lineages. A Friedman test and a Nemenyi
critical difference use the four density methods on their common
complete-informative set, with rank 1 assigned to the higher retention. The
critical-difference constant for four methods at $\alpha=0.05$ is $2.569$.
These are descriptive validation tests. They are not the production
paired-superiority rule, which also requires a frozen evaluator and a
gate-pass analysis. PTF-v1, MFS-v2, and release-safe L3 stay null.

\section{Discussion}
The measurements support a limited statement. The displayed residual,
median $\DopeByteMedian$ bytes, has CatBoost retention $\MedDopeCb$.
CatBoost and the MLP separate it from the three density comparators. The
linear auditor does not. Forest-Flow, on $\NForestCb$ lineages, is ahead
on CatBoost (\WtlForestCb) and the MLP (\WtlForestMlp). CTGAN and TVAE do
not match that retention, and their successful size-$4$ artifacts miss the
cap. None of this is an MFS-v2 score, a PTF-v1 result, or a release-safe
certification. A small Wilcoxon $p$-value does not flip that null. The
8{,}192-step budget moves the CatBoost median by $\DiffSufCb$ and is not
the displayed model. BeyondArena fits $\BeyondPrepared$ further families
inside the cap and does not show retention. TabSyn, a $\PreparedN$-lineage
TabDDPM panel, and an equal tuning budget are not measured. Fit-seed
variance is \FitSeedVariance. Table~\ref{tab:fidelity} is an empirical
ledger, not that production privacy-attack panel.

\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}
\paragraph{Internal.}
The fits use coresets of \CoreMin\ to \CoreMax\ rows, one fit seed, and a
training-derived validation cut of the official training rows. The
supplement records the draw. The official test was not an input to
training, selection, or the numbers in this paper. The informativeness rule
drops a negligible real-versus-null gap. \ThrSentence\ Incomplete groups
are omitted, not zeroed. Chow--Liu and independent marginals are study
code. CTGAN and TVAE use the lineages that finished. Unfinished
scheduling cutoffs are not method failures.

\paragraph{External.}
These fits do not establish behavior on the full tables used by the
diffusion papers, on a five-seed campaign, or on the official test.
Generalization past seed $\SplitSeed$ is \FitSeedVariance. Forest-Flow's
byte comparison uses $\NForestCb$ lineages and the hash-checked fit files.
BeyondArena is a separate fit check. It is not a retention panel, and it is
not pooled with the density block.

\paragraph{Construct.}
Retention is the reported utility. It is not the MFS-v2 scalar, which stays
null. The linear auditor does not separate DOPE from the Gaussian copula.
Table~\ref{tab:fidelity} is not a membership-inference certification. Its
DCR share and DOMIAS AUC are empirical. Empirical resemblance is not
differential privacy and is not HIPAA de-identification.

\paragraph{Statistical.}
The rank tests are descriptive, not the pre-registered production
superiority test. Density, neural, Forest-Flow, and BeyondArena stay
unpooled. A small retention $p$-value does not flip the null scalar.

\section{Conclusion}
On $\NDopeCb$ validation lineages, \texttt{features12\_steps2048} has
CatBoost retention $\MedDopeCb$ (interval $\LoDopeCb$ to $\HiDopeCb$)
and $\DopeWithinCap$ artifacts inside the cap. CatBoost separates it
from the density comparators; the linear auditor does not separate it
from the Gaussian copula. Forest-Flow is ahead on CatBoost
(\WtlForestCb) and the MLP (\WtlForestMlp). The certification scalar
is null, official tests stay sealed, and unrun generators are not measured.
