\begin{abstract}
A generator paper often reports the metric that also selected the configuration, and those native scores live on different scales.
\textbf{D}ownstream \textbf{O}bjective-\textbf{P}reserving \textbf{E}ncoding (DOPE) is the byte-budgeted tabular encoding and the generator that encoding writes.
Retention, the shared utility column, is a null-normalized train-on-synthetic/train-on-real ratio~\citep{esteban2017rcgan}. A retention leaves the release gate closed.
On $\NDopeCb$ informative PMLB regression lineages, fit seed $11$ and synthetic size $4n$, the CatBoost median retention is $\MedDopeCb$ (95\% lineage-bootstrap interval $\LoDopeCb$ to $\HiDopeCb$).
The strongest classical full-panel baseline is author-default ARF. The DOPE generator's retention minus ARF has CatBoost paired median $\DiffArfCb$, with family-cluster bootstrap interval $\LoDiffArfCb$ to $\HiDiffArfCb$ ($n=\NDiffArfCb$; \WtlArfCb).
Inside the density block the strongest tuned baseline is the Gaussian copula. Equivalence stays unclaimed.
Forest-Flow leads on its $\NForestCb$-lineage block, and that Holm test stays non-significant.
MFS-v2 and MFS-v3 stay null. Official tests stay sealed.
The distance ledger stays outside differential privacy and outside HIPAA de-identification.
The $10{,}240$-byte cap is a release gate.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

\vspace{-.1cm}

A generator paper often reports the metric that also selected the
configuration. Those native scores live on different scales: predictive
efficacy for CTGAN and TVAE~\citep{xu2019ctgan}, held-out log density for a
Gaussian copula~\citep{patki2016sdv}, a forest density for
ARF~\citep{watson2023arf}, and a diffusion or CART objective for other
families~\citep{kotelnikov2023tabddpm,nowok2016synthpop}. This paper separates
selection from the common measurement. The supplement states the rule,
the rows, and the budget for each method. CTGAN and TVAE are selected on the
same validation rows and the same linear and MLP auditors that later score
them. Chow--Liu and the independent marginals report no search. After those
choices, the shared columns are retention and the bytes charged to the
artifact. Retention is the null-normalized ratio in \eqref{eq:retention}.
The object under test is the encoding. The ship decision is the release
gate in \cref{sec:kpi}.

\subsection{Downstream objective-preserving encoding}
\label{sec:intro-definition}

\vspace{-.1cm}

DOPE is the byte-budgeted encoding in \cref{sec:method} and the generator
that encoding writes. Figure legends label that generator DOPE. Retention
records how much of a real-trained regressor's improvement over a null is
recovered by an auditor trained on synthetic rows. That ratio is the
TSTR/TRTR comparison named in \cref{sec:related}. The decision those
columns can support is narrow. Retention is the measurement this paper can
finish. A best-generator ranking and a ship decision both stay outside
that column. Master Fitness
v2 (MFS-v2) and MFS-v3, defined in \cref{sec:kpi}, are the release
scores. Both are null on every method here. A missing component or a
failed gate removes the scalar rather than publishing a small number.

\section{Method}
\label{sec:method}
\subsection{Displayed generator}
The displayed profile is the generator this paper reports, and it is the
DOPE artifact named in the abstract and in the figure legends. Its configuration
id is \texttt{features12\_\allowbreak{}steps2048}: at most 12 screened inputs,
a hidden width of 16, a $\tanh$ activation, 2{,}048 AdamW steps at learning
rate $0.002$, a refit readout, and no embedding. It is a compact neural
residual. It stays outside the GAN, diffusion, and transformer families.
The supplement draws that pipeline and states the training freeze and the skip refit.
For a selected input vector $x_S$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:residual}
h_j = \tanh\!\left(b_j + w_j^{\top} x_S\right),
\quad
\hat{y} = a + \beta^{\top} x_S + v^{\top} h .
\end{equation}
There is no embedding table in \eqref{eq:residual}. A separate dataset-action
router uses a vector of dimension $856 + 24\times(128+10) = 4{,}168$. That
vector stays unused for the rows in this paper.

The supplement states the joint draw, the compile search, and the width-and-step grid.
For the displayed DOPE artifacts, the charged byte count is the encoded model
file plus \texttt{projection.json}. The publisher adds those two lengths.
A baseline charge is the length stored for that method.

\subsection{A fixed encoding of the columns}
\label{sec:encoding}
The displayed generator and the dataset encoding both reduce a table to a
fixed set of columns. The budgets differ, and both budgets belong to the
current method.

Training screens columns by absolute rank correlation with the target and
keeps at most 12. Those columns are the support $x_S$ in
\eqref{eq:residual}. The artifact stores their positions in the training
table. That screen is what keeps the residual inside the byte cap. It is a
weak dataset key. A column left unranked by the target is dropped, and a
permutation of the remaining columns changes the stored positions.

The encoding an index can store is the sketch the router already consumes.
\texttt{embed-dataset} min-max normalizes each training column onto the unit
interval, leaves a missing entry missing, and leaves the official
test unread. Each column becomes twelve content summaries: a missing rate, moments,
quantiles, a unique-value share, a tail ratio, and the absolute association
with the target. Columns are sorted by those summaries, variance first, then by the
rest of the summary, then by the sorted observations. A row permutation
leaves the vector fixed. A column permutation leaves it fixed when the
retained set has no cutoff tie; a tie there can change the pairwise
summaries of the retained columns. The width of that sketch is
$10 + 12\times 6 + 64\times 12 + 6 = 856$.
The ten-number header records the task, the log row count, the log column
count, and the shape of the target. Each of the twelve content summaries is
then reduced across columns to a six-number distribution: the minimum, the
lower quartile, the median, the upper quartile, the maximum, and the mean.
That is the $12\times 6$ block. The sketch then keeps the first 64 columns
in the order above and stores each column's twelve summaries, padding a
shorter table with zeros. Absolute correlations among the kept columns
collapse to one further six-number distribution, so the width stays independent of
the square of the column count.

The supplement states how the longer router vector sits on that sketch.
The sketch stays outside the release-score components.

\subsection{Retention}
\label{sec:retention}
Let $L_{\mathrm{null}}$, $L_{\mathrm{real}}$, and
$L_{\mathrm{synthetic}}$ be the auditor's loss on held-out validation rows
when that auditor is trained on a null baseline, on real fit rows, and on
synthetic rows. Those held-out rows come from the official training file.
Retention is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:retention}
\frac{L_{\mathrm{null}}-L_{\mathrm{synthetic}}}{L_{\mathrm{null}}-L_{\mathrm{real}}} .
\end{equation}
The supplement gives the rearrangement of that ratio and an arithmetic example on invented losses.
Equation~\eqref{eq:retention} is a null-normalized TSTR/TRTR
ratio~\citep{esteban2017rcgan}. $L_{\mathrm{synthetic}}$ is the loss of an
auditor trained on synthetic rows and scored on real holdout rows.
$L_{\mathrm{real}}$ is the loss of the same auditor trained on real rows and
scored on those holdout rows. $L_{\mathrm{null}}$ is the null loss on the
holdout. The encoding and the byte-budgeted release gate are the claims.
The ratio is the column they are scored on. Its delta-method variance, and
the reason a small denominator is omitted, is in the supplement.
A value of $1$ means the synthetic-trained auditor matched the real-trained
auditor. A value above $1$ means it beat that auditor. A negative value means
it did worse than the null. A lineage is informative when the real
improvement is at least $0.01$ times the absolute null loss and stays strictly positive.
Otherwise the lineage is omitted. An omitted lineage stays out of the median. The three
auditors are CatBoost~\citep{prokhorenkova2018catboost}, ordinary linear
regression, and an MLP. The lineage score is the median of sample seeds
$101$, $211$, and $307$. The panel score is the median of those lineage
scores. Intervals below are percentile lineage-bootstrap intervals,
10{,}000 resamples of lineages, with the three seeds kept inside the lineage
median. Each bar is that lineage interval.

\subsection{The release score}
\label{sec:kpi}
The decision this project is built to make is whether an artifact may ship.
Retention is the measurement this paper can finish. It is the shared column
after the choices stated in the supplement. A high retention
means an auditor trained on synthetic rows recovered the improvement that
the same auditor obtained from real fit rows. That can be true of an
artifact that is too large to ship, that misses the joint distribution,
that copies training rows, or that moves the features a later model relies
on. The release score keeps those failures apart from a
utility median.

MFS-v2 is that score. With component $c_i\in[0,1]$, weight $w_i>0$,
$\epsilon=10^{-6}$, and $W=\sum_i w_i$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mfs}
\mathrm{MFS\text{-}v2}
= 100\exp\!\left(\frac{1}{W}\sum_i w_i\ln(\epsilon+c_i)\right)
\end{equation}
when the hard-gate map is non-empty, every hard gate passed, and every
positive-weight component is finite and inside $[0,1]$. Otherwise the scalar
is null. The mean is geometric. A component near zero pulls the product
down. A component that is missing, or that falls outside $[0,1]$, removes
the score rather than publishing a small number. An empty gate map, or any
failed hard gate, does the same. Privacy has soft weight zero. A membership
failure, an attribute failure, or a copied row is a gate. Utility cannot
offset it. The weights are utility transfer $0.30$, driver fidelity $0.20$,
distribution fidelity $0.20$, structure fidelity $0.15$, coverage realism
$0.10$, and compactness $0.05$.

The supplement states what each of the six components measures.
Compactness is $\mathrm{clamp}(1 - \mathrm{bytes}/10240,\, 0,\, 1)$. An
artifact past $10{,}240$ bytes fails the byte gate, and the scalar stays
null. Crossing the cap is a failed gate, distinct from a retention of zero.

MFS-v3 is the score a later release has to pass. MFS-v2 is unchanged.
Historical receipts still pin the v2 file. The v3 weights add one
component to the geometric mean. Utility transfer is $0.35$.
Representation closeness, driver fidelity, and distribution fidelity are
each $0.15$. Structure fidelity is $0.10$. Coverage realism and
compactness are each $0.05$. Privacy stays at soft weight zero. A missing
component, a failed gate, a missing prerequisite, or any other weight
vector makes the scalar null. Each component enters as $\max(c_i,\epsilon)$.
With $W=\sum_i w_i$,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:mfsv3}
\mathrm{MFS\text{-}v3}
= \mathrm{clip}_{[0,100]}\!\left(
100\exp\!\left(\frac{1}{W}\sum_i w_i\ln(\max(c_i,\epsilon))\right)
\right)
\end{equation}
when the four prerequisites hold, every hard gate passed, and every
positive-weight component is finite and inside $[0,1]$. Otherwise the
scalar is null. A profile of all ones is $100$ under \eqref{eq:mfsv3}.
The four prerequisites are a complete real-versus-real control, the
required size cells, verified artifact-only sampling, and locked metric
implementations. The Python scorer and the native Rust v3 evaluator both
take $\max(c_i,\epsilon)$ inside the logarithm, clip the result to $[0,100]$,
and withhold the scalar when a prerequisite or a hard gate is missing.
The historical Rust path for \eqref{eq:mfs} remains that v2 formula.
PTF-v1, MFS-v2, and MFS-v3 stay null on every method in these tables.
The cleartext scan stays unscanned where the stored column codes are internal identifiers, so the scalar stays null.
The supplement records the historical placement of $\epsilon$, keeps the illustration out of these tables, and applies the bounded formula to invented components.
A retention stays distinct from the scalar. The compile-time proxy $0.45u + 0.25d + 0.30j$ stays outside
\eqref{eq:mfs} and \eqref{eq:mfsv3}.

\subsection{Statistics}
\label{sec:stats}
Comparisons are paired on the lineages where both methods have a complete
informative group. The tests are two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on the
nonzero paired differences, and one-sided sign tests for a positive
difference, with a matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation.

stays inside its own family. The Wilcoxon signed-rank test targets a
pseudo-median of the nonzero paired differences. The reported estimand is
the sample median and its lineage-bootstrap interval. Those two estimands
