Review base: `aac3fdadf93fdbd07811a0e16e53349b4d8eff40` (2026-10-21).
This is a source/evidence review and a prospective work plan. It does not change

## The story to lead with

**Compile a real table into a small sampling program, then measure what models
still learn.** The research question is the amount of *fitted state* needed to
preserve a declared workload. Normalization and synthetic-row resemblance are
supporting machinery, not the principal contribution.

The strongest current claim is a descriptive size/utility tradeoff on historical
validation. It is not a global compression record. In the generated classical
card, DOPE's selected profile has 1,895 median charged bytes and 0.898 CatBoost
retention; TabSyn's scaled schedule has 42,261,045.5 bytes and 0.924 retention.
Their paired Hodges-Lehmann contrast is approximately -0.003, with an interval
crossing zero: neither superiority nor equivalence follows. This is a ratio of
cohort medians, not a paired distribution of size reductions. The untuned DOPE
default has a lower retention estimate and must remain visible.

Authoritative values and source hashes:
`paper/generated/headline-results.json`,
`paper/generated/legacy-v2-catboost.csv`,
`docs/whitepaper/generated/v2-headline.json` and
`research/benchmark/results/review-fixes-v2/panel.json`.
The separate historical foundation block has 97 matched lineages, not the
classical 99-lineage cohort. Its driver proxy is absolute feature-target
association, not cross-model permutation-driver preservation.

## What is mathematically valuable

Let `g = L_null - L_real > 0`. Retention `T = (L_null - L_syn) / g` has a useful
interpretation: `T >= tau` exactly means
`L_syn - L_real <= (1 - tau) * g`. The permitted loss increase scales with how
much the table improves that learner. It is more comparable across targets than
a universal absolute RMSE tolerance, provided the real-gain floor and uncertainty
gate are satisfied.

The rate-distortion formulation is the right center of gravity:

```text
minimize complete dataset-specific bytes(A), over tested artifacts
subject to lower-bound worst-family transfer >= T90,
           worst-family driver agreement >= D80,
           every required hard gate passing.
```

The finite tested set matters. A sparse byte grid gives a smallest tested
qualifying artifact and a grid interval, not an information-theoretic optimum.
A point at one budget is not TDL. Do not call the artifact size divided into
retention an information density score: such a ratio can favor nearly useless
small artifacts and is not stable near zero gain.

DOPE's compact factorization is substantive: marginals and dependence carry
`p(X)`; a screened residual predictor and conditional noise carry `p(Y | X)`;
the codec turns that model into counted executable state. The novelty must be
located in the combination of *portable bounded sampling programs, complete
charging, and measured cross-learner transfer constraints*. None of one-hot
encoding, min/max scaling, Chow-Liu, copulas, residual networks or heteroscedastic
noise is independently new. Keep the historical method and its later raw,
residual, interaction and neural successors as distinct versioned profiles.

## A stronger mathematical direction: preserve task-relevant moments

A useful next diagnostic is to ask which statistics a learner actually needs,
rather than spending bytes equally on every marginal. For squared loss and a
**fixed** feature map `phi` (including an intercept), write

```math
K_P = E_P[phi(X) phi(X)^T], \quad b_P = E_P[phi(X)Y], \quad c_P = E_P[Y^2].
```

Every linear predictor in that representation has population risk

```math
R_P(beta) = c_P - 2 beta^T b_P + beta^T K_P beta.
```

Thus preserving these moments preserves the whole risk surface for that fixed
predictor class, not just one fitted coefficient vector. For any `||beta|| <= r`,
finite moments give the elementary bound

```math
|R_P(beta) - R_Q(beta)|
  <= |c_P-c_Q| + 2r ||b_P-b_Q||_2 + r^2 ||K_P-K_Q||_op.
```

The proof is expansion of the quadratic, Cauchy-Schwarz for the cross moment,
and the spectral-norm bound for the quadratic form. This is a population
identity/bound for fixed predictors, not a new observed DOPE result or a finite
sample transfer certificate. Training on finite draws adds estimation variance;
conditioning of the moment matrix, regularization and adaptive feature learning
still matter. The statement does not extend automatically to an MLP or a
foundation model.

**Experiment:** freeze several small development feature maps (original numeric
features, grouped one-hot features, a bounded interaction map, and fixed random
features), then measure which moment discrepancies predict lost transfer after
quantization. Test a byte allocator that reduces the most consequential moment
error first, using fit/development rows only. Compare with the current allocator,
a fixed precision codec, and a low-rank-plus-diagonal moment control. The matrix
can cost quadratic space, so count the entire representation and reject a gain
that only comes from a larger uncharged state. Any data-trained feature map or
basis must be carried and charged as well. Keep untouched queries exclusively
for the final evaluator.

This gives the compression objective a concrete workload-specific mechanism to
probe. It could explain where small dependence models work and where they lose
information needed by nonlinear learners. It should be tested before claiming
that general marginal fidelity or a different scaler captures more useful
information.

## The experiments most likely to change the result

These are hypotheses, not predicted wins. First replay existing implemented
successors; do not add a second implementation of the same idea.

| Priority | Hypothesis and comparison | Required evidence / rejection rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | **Interaction-aware screening.** Absolute univariate correlation misses XOR-like and symmetric effects. Compare the existing interaction screen with historical screening while holding encoded bytes and fit time fixed. | Mechanism fixtures plus independent real sources; reject if gains require more uncharged parameters or hurt the worst family |
| P0 | **Residual distribution and cross-fitting.** A conditional mean plus Gaussian noise can erase skew, multimodality or tail risk. Compare the existing no-noise, Student-t, mixture and quantile successors with honest OOF residuals. | Original-unit tail losses, conditional coverage/PIT diagnostics and transfer; no inference that calibrated noise must improve MSE |
| P0 | **Allocate actual bytes to bottlenecks.** A better marginal can matter more than extra target weights, or vice versa. Compare component allocation after serialization, with transform/dictionary overhead included. | A measured equal-byte curve; fit-only selection with development auditors, followed by held-out families |
| P1 | **Task-aware transforms.** Compare affine, support-aware log/asinh and bounded quantile knots. | Fit-fold isolation, inverse reconstruction, clipping/tie/tail counts, original-unit losses and full state charge; no universal scaling winner assumed |
| P1 | **Feature joint model.** Hold the target predictor fixed and ablate independence, tree, copula and richer dependence. | Conditional diagnostics and predictor-only control; marginal resemblance alone is insufficient |
| P1 | **Conditional categorical/missingness model.** Current one-hot encoding and limited split probabilities do not capture arbitrary nominal interactions or missing-not-at-random mechanisms. | Real mixed-type datasets, category relabeling invariance, rare-category coverage and subgroup utility at equal bytes |
| P1 | **Quantization sensitivity.** Compare precision per component and tail-aware knot placement. | Pre/post-codec transfer, stability and error; cap enforced on the complete encoded file, not an estimated weight count |
| P2 | **Cross-auditor selection.** Use worst-family development transfer as the selector rather than one favored learner. | Count every search trial and its cost; hold out entire auditor and source families; no final-test feedback |

Do not change target scaling and declare a win using a transformed loss. For
fixed predictions, affine scaling cancels from MSE retention; nonlinear target
transforms generally change what is optimized. Trees, regularized linear models,
MLPs and context models can respond differently to feature transforms. The
[preprocessing guide](preprocessing.md) states the implementation boundary.

## Benchmark needed for a defensible leading claim

1. **Freeze the estimand first.** Resolve the metric name/contract decisions,
   informative-task calibration, family aggregation, driver stability ceiling,
   multiplicity across budgets and minimum coverage. The candidate protocol
   remains a candidate until those decisions are made; this PR freezes none.
2. **Separate sources, not just rows.** Use a majority of measured real datasets
   in the headline. Group related lineages, duplicates and physical entities;
   use temporal/geographic shifts where relevant. Keep simulated mechanism and
   stress tasks in separate blocks. Audit foundation pretraining overlap.
3. **Publish three tracks.** Author-default, equal total selection/fit compute,
   and equal complete-byte budgets answer different questions. Scaled TabSyn
   schedules and time-capped methods must be labeled. A compressor of fitted
   parameters is not comparable to a saved pipeline whose sidecars are omitted.
4. **Keep strong controls.** Losslessly compressed original data, byte-matched
   row subsets/coresets, predictor-only sampling, independent marginals and
   real-row resampling. Charge row weights, index maps, schema and decoder state.
   Distillation comparisons must state whether they target one learner or many.
5. **Preserve all scheduled outcomes.** Report per-dataset per-budget cells,
   failure rates and informative coverage over fixed denominators. Compare on
   exact shared physical rows; never pool a small successful cohort into a
   larger one or count a missing comparator as a win.
6. **Respect shared randomness.** A global fit seed couples datasets. Use
   shared source-family and global-seed resamples rather than treating each
   lineage/seed combination as independent. Predeclare paired contrasts and
   superiority/noninferiority/equivalence procedures separately.
7. **Show portability and costs.** Reconcile file length, inspector sections,
   full recipient dependency inventory and clean-room reads. Publish compile,
   sample and auditor latency, CPU/GPU seconds, memory and actual bytes. Count
   every failed search attempt.
8. **Separate utility and disclosure.** Run attacks on the released artifact as
   well as samples, including suitable shadow-model membership attacks.
   Empirical diagnostics cannot become a formal DP or de-identification claim.

Required plots after that campaign: (a) per-dataset transfer/driver-versus-bytes
curves with uncertainty and censored budgets; (b) matched paired contrasts on
the locked cohort; (c) complete-byte component bars; (d) quality/coverage/cost
and subgroup/tail diagnostics. Keep today's aggregate historical points
visually separate from those future curves.

## Staged compute request for the PR discussion

Request a development pilot before a broad campaign, with a separately approved
immutable cell manifest. This is not permission to use the sealed corpus.

* **Generation pilot:** 8 rights-cleared real-source datasets × 4 generators
  (DOPE, Gaussian copula, ARF, TabSyn) × 3 fit seeds = 96 fits. DOPE's declared research trainer and TabSyn may use up to 8 GPU-hours total
  under separately admitted GPU profiles. ARF and Gaussian copula receive
  up to 8 CPU-hours. Every fit stops at 600 seconds and the applicable 16 GiB
  GPU limit. Any author schedule that exceeds that cap belongs in the labeled
  compute-matched track, not the author-default track.
* **Auditing:** 3 sample seeds × 2 row counts (`n`, `4n`) × 3 fixed classical
  learner recipes = at most 1,728 synthetic-arm fits. With a 60-second per-fit
  ceiling, plus real/null controls, reserve 32 CPU-hours total. Cap insufficient
  cells terminate as incomplete rather than silently changing the recipe.
* **Scheduling:** request the GPU pilot as at most four separately admitted
  120-minute windows, without expanding the existing weekly lane automatically.
  CPU jobs retain the existing 60-minute job limit. No automatic retries; record
  failures and repair before any new admission. Stop on deadline, memory, rights
  or source-identity failure. Foundation models need a separate qualified
  checkpoint/license/resource request and are outside this pilot allowance.
* **Decision:** establish runnable coverage and variance, then freeze a powered
  successor matrix using the pilot's source-cluster variability. Do not call
  the pilot a record, confirmation or release result. A later five-fit-seed
  campaign needs its own cost estimate and approval.

The preprocessing and residual ablations are a separate development work order;
they are not secretly multiplied into these 96 generation fits.
