\section{Proofs}
\label{app:proofs}
\paragraph{Squared loss.} For any measurable $g$,
$\E_P\Sq{(Y - g(X))} = \E_P\Sq{(Y - m_P(X))} + \E_P\Sq{(m_P(X) - g(X))}$ because the cross term has
conditional mean zero. The null predictor $g \equiv \E_P Y$ gives $s_P + \Var(m_P(X))$. A learner that is
consistent on the support of its training law converges, when trained on the real fit rows, to $m_P$, and,
when trained on enough synthetic rows, to $m_Q$ on the support of $Q_X$; if that support covers the support of
$P_X$, its real test loss converges to $s_P + \E_P\Sq{(m_Q - m_P)}$. Dividing the two gains gives the
proposition. Without the support condition, the synthetic learner's predictions on $P$-mass outside the
support of $Q_X$ are unconstrained, which is the regime of the second corollary.

\paragraph{Flexible learners.} With $e = f_Q - m_Q$ and $w = dP_X/dQ_X$,
$\E_P\Sq{e} = \E_Q[w\,\Sq{e}] \le (\E_Q\Sq{w})^{\One/\Two} (\E_Q e^{\Four})^{\One/\Two}$ by Cauchy--Schwarz,
and $\E_Q\Sq{w} = \One + \chi^{\Two}(P_X\|Q_X)$. A dependence model that reduces the chi-square divergence
tightens the bound for every learner whose fourth moment of error under $Q$ is controlled.

\paragraph{Log loss.} $\E_P[-\log p_Q(Y\mid X)] = \E_P[-\log p_P(Y\mid X)] + \E_P \mathrm{KL}(p_P(\cdot\mid X)\|p_Q(\cdot\mid X))$.
The null predictor attains $H(Y)$ and the Bayes predictor $H(Y\mid X)$, whose difference is $I(X;Y)$.

\paragraph{Equal-byte scaling.} Let $L_k = s_P + C/k$. The real reference with $n$ rows has gain
$\Var(m_P) - C/n$ over the null, and $k$ real rows have gain $\Var(m_P) - C/k$. Setting their ratio to $T$
and solving gives $C/k = (\One - T)\Var(m_P) + T C/n$. Each row costs $(p + \One)$ values of $b$ bits.

\section{Equal-byte study}
\label{app:equal}
\paragraph{Tables.} The historical remainder of the published PMLB panel after its original
name-based family exclusions and three content-duplicate exclusions. This mask
retains simulated pollen and does not establish observed real-world origin.
The mixed/unknown-origin cohort, original records and all numerical results are preserved. \paragraph{Splits and scaling.} A fixed shuffle; \cTestShare{} of the rows, capped at
\cFitCap{}, form the test set and up to \cFitCap{} of the remaining rows the fit set. Every column is
min-max scaled with fit-row statistics, as the historical harness did; heavy-tailed columns are therefore
compressed, which the table workflow's log scaling avoids. \paragraph{Real-row arm.} For each budget on a
power-of-two grid and at each DOPE rung's own median size, the largest uniform subset of fit rows whose
eight- or sixteen-bit codes, sorted by row and stored column-major, fit the budget under the best of three
lossless codecs, plus four bytes of header and the shared scaling charge. The whole table scores as the
real reference. \paragraph{Matched training.} The MLP stops after a fixed number of epochs, so its
optimizer steps scale with the rows it sees, and a subset of a few dozen rows would get a few hundred steps
against thousands for $\cSynthetic$ synthetic rows. In the real-row arm and in the real reference, the MLP
therefore keeps its better test loss of training on the rows as given or resampled with replacement to the
synthetic size. In the resampled variant a tenth of the distinct rows is held out before resampling and
drives early stopping with the auditor's patience, and the best epoch's weights are kept, so validation
never sees a duplicate of a training row. Least squares and CatBoost, with a fixed number of rounds, are
trained as given. Choosing on test rows favors the real rows. \paragraph{DOPE arm.} Five rungs of packed programs, three fit seeds
each, sampled at $\cSynthetic$ rows. \paragraph{Aggregation.} Retention per learner is clipped to the unit
interval; seeds aggregate by the median in both arms; a table's robust value is the smallest over
informative learners; frontiers take the best configuration within each budget; the real-row bytes that
match a level interpolate the real-row frontier in log bytes between bracketing budgets; intervals are
percentile bootstraps over tables. \paragraph{Sensitivity.} Training the real rows as given, the earlier
protocol of this study, gives a gap of \EbAsGivenGap{} [\EbAsGivenGapLo, \EbAsGivenGapHi] (\EbAsGivenWins{}
wins, \EbAsGivenLosses{} losses); leaving the MLP out of the worst-learner score gives \EbNoMlpGap{}
[\EbNoMlpGapLo, \EbNoMlpGapHi] (\EbNoMlpWins{} wins, \EbNoMlpLosses{} losses).

\section{Implementation audit}
\label{app:audit}
Reading the compiler, codec and sampler found the following. Fixed in this release: quantization bits did
not set wire width; CPU builds did not train the published network; a text projection dominated the charge
of small artifacts; no valid dense Gaussian copula was reachable. Open, to keep historical artifacts
reproducible: sequential edge updates on non-tree graphs let latent variances drift, and the vine sampler
ignores conditioning depth; Spearman correlations enter as latent Pearson correlations without the
$\Two\sin(\pi\rho/\Six)$ correction; candidate selection never measures transfer or joint fidelity, so ties go
to the fewest bytes; missingness is completely at random and applied after the target; the binary
calibration map is a binned mean rather than an isotonic fit. The repository's \path{docs/math.md} gives
file references and a research plan.

\section{Compute requested}
\label{app:compute}
Every result here ran on two CPU cores. The experiments that would move the frontier are listed with their
hypotheses in \path{docs/math.md}: an equal-byte study on tables with orders of magnitude more rows, where
the program's advantage should grow; a rate--distortion compile that selects knots, bits, width and inputs by
measured transfer on held-out fit rows; teacher-distilled conditional means; a sensitivity-based bit
allocation; measurement of the driver gate; and a classification panel through the table workflow. The
sealed test should run once after those settle the profile.
