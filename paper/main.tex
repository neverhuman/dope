\begin{abstract}
% Five moves (S2 §17.2). No number appears here until the sealed run exists.
Synthetic data is useful when models learn from it, but utility alone does not say how much
fitted information a generator needs. D.O.P.E.\ (Downstream Objective-Preserving Encoding)
compiles a real table into a bounded sampling program and tests learners trained only on
its synthetic draws against untouched real rows.
We define \MetricLong{} (\MetricName{}): the smallest tested number of dataset-specific
bytes for which learners trained or conditioned only on generated rows recover a declared
share of the real data's predictive gain in every required model family, rely on the same
feature drivers, and pass copy, cleartext and byte-accounting gates, all measured on
untouched real rows. Every data-dependent sampling byte is charged, including transforms
and sidecars. Historical selection-exposed validation is reported separately from the
prospective metric: it illustrates the size--transfer tradeoff but does not establish a
\MetricName{} result. The specified confirmation protocol holds out learner and source
families; the sealed test remains reserved until the qualified panel and analysis plan freeze.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

% Problem: resemblance and single-learner utility ignore generator size.
A dataset is valuable because it changes what a learner can do. We ask how little
executable information can carry that change: compile real training rows into a small
sampler, give only its samples to independent learners, test those learners on untouched
real rows, and count every byte the sampler reads. Train-on-synthetic, test-on-real
evaluation \citep{esteban2017rcgan,jordon2018measuring} supplies the transfer measurement;
this paper makes the charged size of the generator the quantity being reduced and makes
transfer and driver agreement constraints rather than weighted terms.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
(1)~\MetricName{}, a constrained transfer-versus-bytes measure with explicit result states
and hard gates;
(2)~DOPE, a compiler of bounded sampling programs with exact byte charging;
(3)~a cross-model benchmark protocol with qualified auditor families, copy and coreset
controls, and held-out learner and source families;
(4)~executable evidence: charge manifests, golden metric parity between the Rust core and
its Python mirror, and generated tables and figures.

\section{Transfer Description Length}
\label{sec:metric}

For a dataset $d$, an auditor $m$ in family $f$ and an artifact $A$, all losses are paired
on the same untouched real query rows and stated in original target units. Transfer is the
share of the real-data gain over a null that synthetic training recovers:
\begin{equation}
  T_{d,m}(A) = \frac{\Lnull - \Lsyn(A)}{\Lnull - \Lreal}.
  \label{eq:transfer}
\end{equation}
Receipts store $T$ unclipped. A cell is informative only when the real gain clears a
declared floor and its lower confidence bound is positive; an uninformative cell is
excluded from that auditor's estimate, never scored as zero. The null is the strongest
baseline that learns nothing from the table, including no-context and label-shuffled
context for in-context foundation models.

Within a family the transfer values are averaged; across the required families the
worst family is kept, $\Trobust(A) = \min_f T_f(A)$. Driver fidelity $\Drobust$ is built
the same way from per-auditor driver scores (Section~\ref{sec:drivers}). An artifact
qualifies when
\begin{equation}
  \LCB\bigl(\Trobust(A)\bigr) \ge \tau_T, \qquad \Drobust(A) \ge \tau_D, \qquad G(A) = \text{pass},
  \label{eq:qualify}
\end{equation}
where $G$ is the conjunction of hard gates. \MetricName{} is the charged dataset-specific
payload of the qualifying artifact at the smallest tested budget, reported with its grid
interval. It is the smallest \emph{tested} size, not an optimum. The headline operating
point is \HeadlinePoint{} ($\tau_T = $ \TauT, $\tau_D = $ \TauD) in the \ContractState{} contract
\ContractId; the budget grid is \ByteGrid. When no tested budget qualifies, the result is \emph{not reached in the tested
range}; when a required cell is missing, no number is emitted.

\paragraph{Why this operating point matters.}
For a positive real-data gain, requiring $T \geq \tau_T$ bounds
the excess loss paid for synthetic training:
\begin{equation}
  \Lsyn(A)-\Lreal \leq (1-\tau_T)(\Lnull-\Lreal).
  \label{eq:excess}
\end{equation}
The allowed distortion is relative to what the real table teaches that learner,
not a fixed error tolerance shared across unrelated targets. The rate is the charged
length $b(A)$ of the fitted program. Over the finite set of tested artifacts,
\begin{equation}
  \operatorname{TDL} = \min_{A\in\mathcal{A}_{\rm tested}} b(A)
  \quad\text{subject to Equation~\eqref{eq:qualify}.}
\end{equation}
This separates the objective from the constraints: a larger model cannot compensate for a
failed gate by a weighted score. It also avoids a retention-per-byte ratio that can reward
a tiny but useless generator. Testing more budgets requires a frozen multiplicity and
selection procedure; it does not permit choosing a point on the sealed queries.

\section{The DOPE sampling program}
\label{sec:method}

The historical compiler factorizes the sampling model into a feature distribution and a
conditional target model, $p_A(x,y)=p_\phi(x)q_\theta(y\mid x)$. Quantized feature marginals
and a compact dependence family model $p_\phi$: candidate families include independence,
Chow--Liu, Gaussian copula, autoregressive, factor, vine and mixture structures. Marginal
Gaussianization is an internal coordinate system, not a requirement that physical data be
Gaussian. A screened subset of features $x_S$ enters a residual predictor,
\begin{equation}
  \widehat y(x)=a+\beta^{\mathsf T}x_S+
  v^{\mathsf T}\tanh(b+Wx_S),\qquad
  y=\widehat y(x)+\sigma(x)\varepsilon.
  \label{eq:sampler}
\end{equation}
The noise scale is fitted to residual magnitude. The historical profile uses Gaussian noise
and a bounded target path; the native raw-unit and residual successors are distinct,
explicitly versioned profiles. Results from those profiles must not be pooled under a
single algorithm label. Historical capacity, optimization steps and the quantized codec
are recorded in the retained validation paper and source, rather than redefined here.

\paragraph{Compile, reload, evaluate.}
Only fit rows reach transforms, feature screening, dependence fitting and target fitting.
Candidate structures are quantized and encoded before their complete size is compared
with the budget. The compiler's fit-time proxy is not \MetricName{} and cannot certify
held-out transfer. Sampling reloads retained state after fitting exits. Independent
auditors see only draws for training or context; the evaluator alone holds real query
targets. A predictor-only control tests whether useful transfer requires the feature
joint distribution in addition to a stored regressor.

\paragraph{Mixed types and units.}
Numeric affine state is fitted on training rows only and inverted before raw-unit
evaluation. Nominal categories use column-scoped one-hot features with explicit missing
and unknown states in the bounded mixed carrier. That carrier currently has limited
conditional category modeling; its existence is not a mixed-type benchmark result.
Learned category maps and inverse transforms count as payload. Group derived columns
under their original feature for attribution. Nonlinear target transforms change the
training objective and require separate original-unit comparisons.

\section{Byte accounting}
\label{sec:bytes}

The primary rate is the dataset-specific payload: every byte whose value depends on the
fitted data and that sampling reads, including schema, transforms, category dictionaries,
seeds needed for replay, framing and sidecars. The sampling closure, which adds the decoder,
runtime and any shared checkpoint a cold sampler opens, is reported beside it. Bytes come
from one canonical container and a charge manifest; the compiler, the inspector, the file
length, the sum of charged sections and a traced clean-room sampler must agree. Encoded
value counts are diagnostics and never rank methods.

\section{Auditors}
\label{sec:auditors}

Auditors come from a registry with a typed qualification path (specified, source verified,
weights verified, environment verified, parity verified, resource verified, qualified).
The required families are boosted trees \citep{prokhorenkova2018catboost}, task-trained
neural models, and linear or additive models; tabular foundation models form the held-out
confirmation family that the compiler never sees. Only qualified auditors count. The tabular foundation models named in the registry (Kumo Tabular,
Mitra-v2, TabICLv2, TabPFN, LimiX, TabDPT, TabFM) are planned and unqualified in the TDL-v1 registry at the time
of writing, and no TDL-v1 result in this draft uses them.

\section{Driver fidelity}
\label{sec:drivers}

For each qualified auditor, a real-trained and a synthetic-trained instance of the same
recipe are explained on the same real test rows by grouped permutation importance over the
original features. Mass agreement (one minus total variation), rank agreement and sign
agreement on stable drivers are each divided by the agreement two real-trained instances
reach, so a synthetic table is never held to a standard real data cannot meet. The auditor
score is the smallest normalized component, the family score their mean, and $\Drobust$ the
worst family. Raw attribution magnitudes are never compared across model families.

\section{Benchmark protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

Real tables form the majority of the headline cohort; shift, mechanism and stress cohorts
are reported separately. Three tracks compare methods at their author defaults, at equal
selection compute, and at equal byte budgets. Controls include copied rows, a compressed
copy of the original table as a size reference, a row-subset frontier, predictor-only and
independent-marginal samplers. The seed design is \SeedDesign. Every scheduled cell ends in
a terminal disposition, and failures stay in every denominator. The sealed test is opened
once through a frozen release manifest.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\MetricName{} is an operational rate--distortion measure for one declared supervised
workload. It does not estimate Shannon information or Kolmogorov complexity, it is not a
code-length optimum, and it makes no causal claim. The artifact is conditioned on one
target and does not replace the table for other uses. Empirical attack results are not
formal differential privacy and not HIPAA de-identification. Foundation-model auditors
drift and carry license terms that can block their use.

\paragraph{Failure modes that matter.}
Univariate screening can miss interaction-only signals; a small target predictor may
preserve means while losing conditional tails. Marginal Gaussianization does not recover
arbitrary dependence, and independent mask draws do not establish realistic missingness.
Quantization can erase rare effects, while unstable real-data lift makes a retention ratio
unreliable. Correlated substitute features can also change attribution without changing
predictions. These are falsifiable research questions for a byte-matched ablation panel,
not improvements inferred from a favorable aggregate. The supplement specifies the
tests and separates them from the frozen confirmation run.

\section{Claim ladder}
\label{sec:ladder}

Every table and figure carries one evidence label: development, matched benchmark, sealed
final, or release-certified. This draft is at the development rung. Superiority, cross-model
and driver-preservation statements are pending their evidence and are not made here.

\paragraph{Related work.}
Generators for tabular data range from copulas, dependence trees and synthesis by
sequential models \citep{chow1968dependence,patki2016sdv,nowok2016synthpop} to adversarial
\citep{xu2019ctgan}, forest-based \citep{watson2023arf} and diffusion \citep{kotelnikov2023tabddpm}
models, including latent synthesis and joint mixed-type diffusion
\citep{zhang2024tabsyn,shi2025tabdiff}. Dataset distillation and model distillation compress data or models for one
learner \citep{wang2018distillation,hinton2015distilling}; generative compression names learned
media codecs \citep{santurkar2017generative}. Description-length and bottleneck ideas
\citep{rissanen1978modeling,tishby2000bottleneck} motivate the framing but are not claimed as
the measure. Privacy evaluation of synthetic data is discussed by
\citet{stadler2022groundhog} and \citet{platzer2021holdout}.
