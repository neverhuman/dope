\begin{abstract}
% Five moves (S2 §17.2). No number appears here until the sealed run exists.
Synthetic tables are usually judged by how much they resemble the source or by one
downstream learner, while the size of the generator that produced them goes uncounted.
We define \MetricLong{} (\MetricName{}): the smallest tested number of dataset-specific
bytes for which learners trained or conditioned only on generated rows recover a declared
share of the real data's predictive gain in every required model family, rely on the same
feature drivers, and pass copy, cleartext and byte-accounting gates, all measured on
untouched real rows. DOPE (Downstream Objective-Preserving Encoding) compiles a table into
a bounded executable sampling program whose every sampling byte is charged. The specified
protocol holds out real rows, learner families and source families, and reports the full
transfer-versus-bytes frontier rather than a weighted score.

\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

% Problem: resemblance and single-learner utility ignore generator size.
A dataset is valuable because it changes what a learner can do. We ask how little
executable information can carry that change: compile real training rows into a small
sampler, give only its samples to independent learners, test those learners on untouched
real rows, and count every byte the sampler reads. Train-on-synthetic, test-on-real
evaluation \citep{esteban2017rcgan,jordon2018measuring} supplies the transfer measurement;
this paper makes the charged size of the generator the quantity being reduced and makes
transfer and driver agreement constraints rather than weighted terms.

\section{Transfer Description Length}
\label{sec:metric}

For a dataset $d$, an auditor $m$ in family $f$ and an artifact $A$, all losses are paired
on the same untouched real query rows and stated in original target units. Transfer is the
share of the real-data gain over a null that synthetic training recovers:
\begin{equation}
  T_{d,m}(A) = \frac{\Lnull - \Lsyn(A)}{\Lnull - \Lreal}.
  \label{eq:transfer}
\end{equation}
Receipts store $T$ unclipped. A cell is informative only when the real gain clears a
declared floor and its lower confidence bound is positive; an uninformative cell is
excluded from that auditor's estimate, never scored as zero. The null is the strongest
baseline that learns nothing from the table, including no-context and label-shuffled
context for in-context foundation models.

Within a family the transfer values are averaged; across the required families the
worst family is kept, $\Trobust(A) = \min_f T_f(A)$. Driver fidelity $\Drobust$ is built
the same way from per-auditor driver scores (Section~\ref{sec:drivers}). An artifact
qualifies when
\begin{equation}
  \LCB\bigl(\Trobust(A)\bigr) \ge \tau_T, \qquad \Drobust(A) \ge \tau_D, \qquad G(A) = \text{pass},
  \label{eq:qualify}
\end{equation}
where $G$ is the conjunction of hard gates. \MetricName{} is the charged dataset-specific
payload of the qualifying artifact at the smallest tested budget, reported with its grid
interval. It is the smallest \emph{tested} size, not an optimum. The headline operating
point is \HeadlinePoint{} ($\tau_T = $ \TauT, $\tau_D = $ \TauD); the budget grid is
\ByteGrid. When no tested budget qualifies, the result is \emph{not reached in the tested
range}; when a required cell is missing, no number is emitted.

\section{Byte accounting}
\label{sec:bytes}

The primary rate is the dataset-specific payload: every byte whose value depends on the
fitted data and that sampling reads, including schema, transforms, category dictionaries,
seeds needed for replay, framing and sidecars. The sampling closure, which adds the decoder,
runtime and any shared checkpoint a cold sampler opens, is reported beside it. Bytes come
from one canonical container and a charge manifest; the compiler, the inspector, the file
length, the sum of charged sections and a traced clean-room sampler must agree. Encoded
value counts are diagnostics and never rank methods. \pending{Section-byte ablation and

\section{Auditors}
\label{sec:auditors}

Auditors come from a registry with a typed qualification path (specified, source verified,
weights verified, environment verified, parity verified, resource verified, qualified).
The required families are boosted trees \citep{prokhorenkova2018catboost}, task-trained
neural models, linear or additive models, and tabular foundation models. Only qualified
auditors count. The tabular foundation models named in the registry (Kumo Tabular,
Mitra-v2, TabICLv2, TabPFN, LimiX, TabDPT, TabFM) are planned and unqualified at the time
of writing, and no result in this draft uses them. \pending{Qualified auditor table from

\section{Driver fidelity}
\label{sec:drivers}

For each qualified auditor, a real-trained and a synthetic-trained instance of the same
recipe are explained on the same real test rows by grouped permutation importance over the
original features. Mass agreement (one minus total variation), rank agreement and sign
agreement on stable drivers are each divided by the agreement two real-trained instances
reach, so a synthetic table is never held to a standard real data cannot meet. The auditor
score is the smallest normalized component, the family score their mean, and $\Drobust$ the
worst family. Raw attribution magnitudes are never compared across model families.

\section{Benchmark protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

Real tables form the majority of the headline cohort; shift, mechanism and stress cohorts
are reported separately. Three tracks compare methods at their author defaults, at equal
selection compute, and at equal byte budgets. Controls include copied rows, a compressed
copy of the original table as a size reference, a row-subset frontier, predictor-only and
independent-marginal samplers. The seed design is \SeedDesign. Every scheduled cell ends in
a terminal disposition, and failures stay in every denominator. The sealed test is opened
once through a frozen release manifest.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\MetricName{} is an operational rate--distortion measure for one declared supervised
workload. It does not estimate Shannon information or Kolmogorov complexity, it is not a
code-length optimum, and it makes no causal claim. The artifact is conditioned on one
target and does not replace the table for other uses. Empirical attack results are not
formal differential privacy and not HIPAA de-identification. Foundation-model auditors
drift and carry license terms that can block their use.

\section{Claim ladder}
\label{sec:ladder}

Every table and figure carries one evidence label: development, matched benchmark, sealed
final, or release-certified. This draft is at the development rung. Superiority, cross-model
and driver-preservation statements are pending their evidence and are not made here.

\paragraph{Related work.}
Generators for tabular data range from copulas, dependence trees and synthesis by
sequential models \citep{chow1968dependence,patki2016sdv,nowok2016synthpop} to adversarial
\citep{xu2019ctgan}, forest-based \citep{watson2023arf} and diffusion \citep{kotelnikov2023tabddpm}
models. Dataset distillation and model distillation compress data or models for one
learner \citep{wang2018distillation,hinton2015distilling}; generative compression names learned
media codecs \citep{santurkar2017generative}. Description-length and bottleneck ideas
\citep{rissanen1978modeling,tishby2000bottleneck} motivate the framing but are not claimed as
the measure. Privacy evaluation of synthetic data is discussed by
\citet{stadler2022groundhog} and \citet{platzer2021holdout}.
