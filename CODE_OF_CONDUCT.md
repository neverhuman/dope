# Code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Welcome different experience levels, explain
technical disagreements with evidence, and give credit for contributions.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, and
publication of another person's private information are unacceptable in project
issues, reviews, discussions and project events.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, limit access,
or exclude repeated or severe misconduct. Apply these standards consistently and
consider both impact and context. Criticism of an implementation or scientific
claim is welcome; attacks on a person are not.

Report misconduct privately to the owner, Jepson Taylor, through the contact
options on the [maintainer profile](https://github.com/jepsontaylor). If the report
concerns the owner, contact another repository maintainer listed in recent
reviews. Ask for a private channel before sharing sensitive details. Maintainers
protect reporter privacy to the extent possible, disclose conflicts of interest,
and explain decisions to the affected participants where appropriate.
